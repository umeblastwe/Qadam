Why we named it Qadam

Qadam means a step. Every pair we sell is chosen for the steps of an ordinary day: to the office, to class, to a wedding, to the market.

What we sell

Everyday sneakers, formal leather shoes, sandals and flats, plus traditional Pakistani footwear like khussa and Peshawari chappal. Between PKR 2,900 and PKR 5,800.

How we sell

Qadam is an online-only store. We take orders on the website, WhatsApp and Instagram, pack them ourselves and ship across Pakistan by courier.

Who it is for

Students, young professionals and families who want good-looking, comfortable shoes at fair prices without visiting a mall.
